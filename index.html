Console

Load your copy of the game SD card: the folder (or a zip of it) that contains the game folder (e.g. pirates/). The files stay on this device; nothing is uploaded. An emulator/ folder next to it may hold board.png, console_overlay.png, board-antennas.json and settings.json; they are used automatically.

Controls
  • Figures: drag-and-drop one onto an antenna area, or tap it and then tap the area. Drag-and-drop it back into the drawer to take it off the board.
  • Press the buttons around the LED display.
  • Tap the LED display to enlarge the console; tap it or next to it to shrink it again.
  • Gear button (bottom left of the board): these settings. Button at the bottom right: the “Off The Board” drawer with the figures.
Board & Pictures

Photos of your own board and console overlay sheet, and where the antennas sit on the board (a .json file, see examples/board-antennas-example.json). The files stay on this device; nothing is uploaded. The ones in the card's emulator/ folder are loaded with the card.
